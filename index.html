Sim-to-real policy learning on a desktop arm

Started September 2026. Personal project.

Train a policy in NVIDIA Isaac Lab, move it onto a physical SO-ARM101 arm, and measure how much of the simulated performance survives the transfer. Then collect teleoperated demonstrations with LeRobot and train an imitation-learning policy on a pick-and-place task.

Platform

SO-ARM101 arm (LeRobot kit). Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab for training on a desktop with an RTX 5070 Ti.

Done

Arm assembled. Setting up LeRobot on Linux and the Isaac Lab reach task.

Next

Train a reach policy in Isaac Lab, transfer it to the real arm, and log success rate over repeated trials. Add domain randomization if the sim-to-real gap is large.

Plan

Teleop demonstrations through LeRobot, then an imitation-learning policy (ACT or Diffusion Policy) for pick-and-place, evaluated the same way.

Autonomous rover: detect, track, drive

KBR, data science intern, June to August 2026. Multi-Domain Autonomous Command and Control project.

A ground rover that finds a target with its camera, keeps it in frame, and drives to it on its own, built for safety-operations use. It also carries an on-device voice agent so a person can talk to the robot without any cloud connection.

Platform

Jetson Orin NX, Astra Pro Plus RGB-D camera, ReSpeaker mic array, Ackermann-steered chassis

Stack

ROS 2 Humble. YOLO26 exported to ONNX and TensorRT FP16. Single-target tracker. 20 Hz PID approach loop with IMU gyro feedback driving the steering. Local LLM voice agent for medical triage questions.

My part

Built the detection-to-steering loop and the voice pipeline, and tuned both on the vehicle.

Result

Closed loop from camera frame to steering command at 20 Hz, running on the Jetson.

Learned perception: a sonar-to-vision transformer

Independent research, December 2025 to present.

Underwater robots lose their cameras in murky water. This transformer maps imaging-sonar returns into vision-style features so an AcTag fiducial detector keeps working when the cameras cannot see.

Platform

Jetson Orin Nano

Stack

PyTorch training. OpenCV and NumPy preprocessing to propose tag candidates. ONNX to TensorRT FP16. ROS 2 with tf2 and the robot_localization EKF.

Status

Running in FP16 on the Orin Nano. Detections feed the EKF for loop closure and pose estimation.

Result

Autonomous-fleet telemetry pipeline

Forterra, yard software intern, 2025.

Autonomous yard vehicles produce a steady stream of telemetry. I built the pipeline that lands it in a database and the dashboards the team uses to see how the fleet is doing. Fleet learning starts with exactly this kind of logging: you cannot train on what you never recorded.

Stack

Dockerized Python ETL streaming into PostgreSQL with idempotent upserts and fault-tolerant batching. Grafana dashboards.

Result

Live views of vehicle uptime, autonomous versus manual miles, and mission success.
